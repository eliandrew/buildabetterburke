Make the connection.

Explore the connection between the Ship Canal Trail, Fremont Bridge, and Burke-Gilman Trail through eight study sections, nearby businesses, observations, ideas, and photos.

Explore the route

Select a line or number to view a section. Choose a business marker for its location, priorities, supported solutions, and conversation notes. Discussion prompts identify questions to investigate; they are not verified issues, approved designs, or statements of stakeholder support.

Map sources & boundaries

The offline map uses OpenStreetMap geometry retrieved November 12, 2026. Study-section lines and the corner boundaries are for discussion, not surveyed limits. The ramp follows OSM way 41288884. The current discussion focuses on the south-side connection and the bridge. North-side connections are reserved for a later phase.

The aerial reference is the supplied Google screenshot, with its original attribution retained. It covers only part of the map and is approximately aligned. Outside the screenshot, the illustrated map remains visible.

Take a closer look

Switch between the map and aerial reference, zoom in, or open a photo for more detail. Print view prints the selected section and information tab. This map can also be shared as a single HTML file and opened offline.
